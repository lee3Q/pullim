# Perplexity 리서치 프롬프트

## 용도
솔로 파운더 AI 활용 실전 사례 + 수치 수집

## 프롬프트 원문
```
I'm a solo founder (no team members) building an AI-powered mental health/decision-making SaaS product.

I previously tried simulating a virtual AI team (CTO, PM, AI Lead, Safety expert, etc.) using LLM agents, but it failed:
- Hallucinations in cross-validation (agents agreeing with each other without real verification)
- No genuine diverse perspectives — just one model roleplaying multiple people
- MCP-based multi-session agents also failed (stateless, no real context persistence)

Research questions:
1. What are proven AI utilization patterns for solo founders in 2025-2026? (not AI teams, but actual workflows)
2. What are the failure modes of "AI team simulation" and why do they fail?
3. How do successful solo founders use AI for: product decisions, technical architecture, user research, go-to-market?
4. Are there specific tools, frameworks, or workflows recommended for solo AI-native founders?
5. Case studies of solo founders who shipped AI products — what was their actual workflow?

Focus on practical, evidence-based answers with sources. Avoid hype.
```

## 결과 품질
