## English Description

### Short Description (80 chars)
AI doesn't give answers. It asks questions to help you untangle your thoughts.

### Full Description
**Ever had a worry you couldn't quite talk to anyone about?**

Pullim is a self-understanding coaching service where AI doesn't give you answers — it asks the right questions so you can figure things out yourself.

**Who it's for**
• People with worries they can't organize
• Too much for journaling alone, too little for therapy
• Those who need "What do I actually want?" before "What should I do?"

**How it works**
• 3 worlds (Adventurer's Forest / Moonlit Garden / Strategy Room)
• 5-step ladder: Sensation → Emotion → Core → Reframe → Action
• AI reads your choices in real-time and shows its thinking transparently
• Hit the cheat button anytime to change direction

**What Pullim doesn't do**
• No diagnosis
• No advice-giving
• Not a medical service

Crisis situations are immediately redirected to professional resources.
